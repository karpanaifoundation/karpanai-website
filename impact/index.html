The child who created a Pink World

A 13-year-old student wrote and published The Pink World - a story set in a world where everyone is born pink except one blue child. Through this simple metaphor, the student explored:

Who gets to belong? What happens when someone is treated differently? What does equality really mean? How do we fight for freedom?

What made the story remarkable was not simply that a child became a published author. It was that the student took a complex Social Science idea and made it his own. He moved beyond memorising the word equality and began exploring identity, exclusion, discrimination and social justice through imagination.
